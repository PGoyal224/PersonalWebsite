My Projects

Dynamic Nutrition Orchestrator

In progress: Meal planning can be difficult for individuals with dietary restrictions because they have to consider their health needs while still enjoying their food. Often these restrictions can be difficult to manage, and it can be hard to find recipes that fit within those restrictions. This project aims to create a meal planning application that will help individuals with dietary restrictions plan their meals more easily based on their targets for macronutrients and cultural preferences.

Medtronic: Emulator for Testing and Demonstrating Monitor Functionality

I developed a cross-platform emulator using Dart/Flutter that connects to a Medtronic monitor and displays patient data for a variety of different scenarios for testing and field-demonstration purposes. My application reduced simulator setup time by 80–90% by replacing a hardware-dependent system with an MQTT emulator, enabling faster, more reliable testing and automated testing workflows.

BTT Machine Learning Foundations Capstone Project: Predicting U.S. Salary Brackets

I explored different machine learning techniques by developing a decision tree and neural network model to predict salary brackets using U.S. Census data. After initial training and testing, both models achieved 85% accuracy. I recommended the decision tree approach based on its interpretability and lower computational cost.
